`work/rebuild_month.py`: every UR720/UR722 (Harare–Lusaka), UR334
(Zanzibar–Kilimanjaro), UR206–UR209 (Nairobi A330 pattern) and UR342/UR343

UR209 ×14, UR206 ×11, UR207 ×11, UR342 ×9, UR343 ×9.

The FTA cross-check (`data_gaps.md`, from `work/build_october_fta.py`)
shows the middle sector was where the FR24 *export* lost coverage:
UR720 LUN-HRE, UR722 HRE-LUN and UR334 ZNZ-JRO legs were missing while
the day's outbound and closing sectors were present. On 2026-10-23
those tracks were recovered from FR24 playback (28 October tracks),
and the fresh export of 2026-10-23 extended coverage through 22 Oct;
the few triangle plan legs still without a track did not operate.
Note the recovered 21 Oct UR720/UR722 sectors flew late as UGD722D,
and the 22 Oct UR722X rotation — filed NON_SCHEDULED — operated
EBB–HRE–EBB anyway (in `flights.csv`).

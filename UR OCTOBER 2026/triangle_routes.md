`work/rebuild_month.py`: every UR720/UR722 (Harare–Lusaka), UR334
(Zanzibar–Kilimanjaro), UR206–UR209 (Nairobi A330 pattern) and UR342/UR343

shows the middle sector was where the FR24 *export* lost coverage:

the day's outbound and closing sectors were present. On 2026-10-23

the few triangle plan legs still without a track did not operate.

# Schedule delays — September 2026

No `schedule_delays.csv` is produced for September in this rebuild.

September punctuality (`punctuality.csv` / `punctuality.md`) keeps the
frozen build's basis — the FR24 app status dot plus delay against the
service's own usual time — because the app captures exist for September
and were the better source. A timetable-basis file like October's would
require transcribing the 30 FTA day-plans for September (the October
folder shows the worked example: `work/fta_oct2026.csv` +
`work/build_october_fta.py`). Until that transcription is done, September
has no honest STD/STA baseline, and inventing one from operated times
would answer the scheduling question with the operation's own habits.

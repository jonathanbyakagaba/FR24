# Triangle routes — September 2026 (rebuilt 2026-10-20)

`triangle_assignments.csv` (225 sector rows) was regenerated by
`work/rebuild_month.py`: every UR720/UR722 (Harare–Lusaka), UR334
(Zanzibar–Kilimanjaro), UR206–UR209 (Nairobi A330 pattern) and UR342/UR343
(Mombasa) sector in the rebuilt `flights.csv`, with the tail attached from
`tails.csv` where the app-based assignment exists (600+ flights matched).

Sector counts in September: UR722 ×48, UR720 ×40, UR334 ×37, UR208 ×20,
UR209 ×19, UR207 ×18, UR206/UR342/UR343 making up the rest.

What to look for: the **middle sector is the fragile one**. Where a triangle
sector has no row here but the day's outbound and closing sectors exist, the
middle sector flew without a usable track — the same pattern the October
FTA cross-check (October folder's `data_gaps.md`) confirms against the
operator's plan. September had no FTA transcription in this rebuild, so
September triangle gaps are read from the track record alone.

Note the frozen build's triangle file was cumulative from 29 Aug; this one
is scoped to the September zip (sectors dated by origin-local departure).
